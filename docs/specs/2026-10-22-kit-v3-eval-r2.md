## Verdict

Review quality is **not** the problem: 13/13 recall at `high` on both models, 12½/13 for sonnet alone at the
default effort, zero hard false positives at `high` and one at `medium`, cross-model `CONFIRMED` behaving as
designed. What leaks is **reliability of the agent output contract** — wrong anchors in all four agents,
bundled blocks and the format skipped on sonnet when it sits behind an external path — and the main session
(opus) silently pays to repair it. F1–F5 and F9 are text edits that close exactly that; F7 is a round-1
leftover; K9/F3 waits for the owner. **A round 3 is not warranted as a full evaluation.** Warranted instead:
the two headless reruns in F1/F9 on the same fixture, one of them under the kit's own CLAUDE.md (≈ $1.4), to
confirm anchors, block shape and column compliance on sonnet at medium — and nothing else: a third read of
the whole kit would reconfirm rounds 1 and 2.
